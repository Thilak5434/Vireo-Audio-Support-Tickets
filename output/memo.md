# Memo: Vireo Audio support tickets

To: Priya Raman, Head of Customer Experience

The tool turns 11,875 canonical tickets into a weekly complaint digest and a closure-volume leaderboard. It removes 653 migration duplicate rows and keeps the warranty team out of Tier 1 volume comparisons.

The main opportunity is repeat contact: 1,516 tickets (12.8%) matched the policy proxy of the same customer and issue returning within 30 days. A 20% reduction would be worth about Rs 14,655 per quarter at the policy rate of Rs 290 per contact. This is the number to manage, not a claim that the tool itself guarantees savings.

The leaderboard is deliberately labelled workload, not performance. Warranty cases are multi-touch and must be measured in resolution days. The digest uses a fixed local taxonomy, so it has no per-ticket model bill.

Next action: review the top weekly issues with product, logistics, and returns owners, choose one repeat-contact issue, and compare its rate after one quarter.
